## Implementation and formula
The additive historicalNeighbors contract is exposed by /api/odds-intelligence/:matchId and list analysis, attached as explanatory gate evidence, and rendered in the Turkish Geçmiş Komşular panel. Legacy pastTwins/resultMap fields remain available. Each pastTwin also receives similarityScore/quality/breakdown. Legacy payloads remain renderable, with unavailable reliability explicitly identified.

Similarity = market(30) + odds(0–30) + league(0–20) + context(0–20).
Exact type/name/selection/line incompatibility rejects the candidate. Odds points average clamp(1-absolute_difference/0.5,0,1) for opening and latest genuine decimal odds. Context points use existing opening/latest fair probabilities with the existing 0.12 scale. Same competition earns 20; a different competition with the same known country earns 10; cross-country earns zero. Same country is only geographic context, not a claim of competitive equivalence. Unknown competition gives null league points and earns no points. Missing numerical components are null and earn no points; available components are never rescaled upward. No rating/ranking or favorite team for total markets is invented. The original route-distance dimensions remain visible for audit; ranking uses contextual score first, legacy distance/date/ID as tie-breakers. Existing +/-5% odds-band membership is retained. Match IDs are deduplicated.

Policy constants are centralized in neighborReliabilityConfig: HIGH >=85, MEDIUM >=70, LOW <70; usable settled samples 0–2 INSUFFICIENT, 3–4 LOW_SAMPLE, 5–9 MODERATE_SAMPLE, 10+ USABLE_SAMPLE. Fewer than five usable results gives INSUFFICIENT_DATA; if at least five settled raw samples exist but fewer than five are usable, it gives WEAK_EVIDENCE. Five usable samples permit MODERATE. More than half of usable samples being cross-league or unknown league forces WEAK. STRONG requires at least ten usable samples, average >=85, at least 80% HIGH and no excessive cross-league reliance. Outcome frequency never affects this state.

sampleSize counts visible distinct candidates. settledSampleSize=wins+losses excludes missing/unsupported settlement. rawWinRate is wins/settledSampleSize*100. usableSampleSize includes only settled HIGH/MEDIUM results. weightedWinRate=sum(score for usable wins)/sum(score for all usable results)*100. averageSimilarityScore explicitly has scope USABLE_SETTLED. No denominator gives null, never 0%. Weighted rates and percentages for fewer than three settled results are suppressed in UI, with counts and insufficient warning retained. LOW samples remain visible in the inspector. Raw result-map cards also use count-plus-warning formatting.

## Prediction Gate interaction
No files in src/predictions changed. Evidence is explanatory; insufficient/weak evidence has supportingEvidence=NONE, moderate LIMITED, strong NORMAL. These labels do not modify candidate scores. Legacy evidence strength is capped by the new status, and weak/insufficient ODDS_TWINS conflict signals become UNAVAILABLE. OFFICIAL still requires independently satisfied gates and LOCKED_PREDICTION. PREVIEW remains WAITING. Existing thresholds, shared complete-state policy and execution/AI authority remain unchanged/false. No outcome-driven fitting was performed.

## Tests and limitations
Regression suite includes live, recommendations, control audit, data coverage, match detail and Prediction Gate. Added deterministic tests cover 1/1, 2/2, all four evidence states, exact mismatches, score bounds and summation, country/league penalties, missing data, weighted denominators, duplicate/future exclusion, API serialization, escaped Turkish UI and independent lock requirements. PostgreSQL regression additionally checks the new contract from actual snapshot rows and unchanged prediction journal counts.

Local typecheck, lint and unit tests pass. Full npm test was run; this machine has no working container runtime, so its PostgreSQL test suite could not start. GitHub CI supplies PostgreSQL validation. No production data import, deployment or merge is part of this task.

Remaining limits: default nearest-neighbor panel is capped at 20 (band 100), so these are selected-neighbor counts, not the whole archive. Similarity is a deterministic evidence heuristic, not a calibrated outcome probability. Country is optional; ranking/rating and explicit total-market favorite strength are unavailable. Unsupported outcome definitions, including quarter-line settlement, remain UNAVAILABLE. Legacy result maps are transparent raw distributions, not confidence. No change to separate Prediction V1 historical model/strategy or its odds-similarity dataset.
